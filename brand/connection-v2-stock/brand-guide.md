# Creator Cash Flow

## Connection identity · version 2 — stock photography

Brand idea: **Good work. Connected.**

Creator Cash Flow brings creators, agencies and brands into one shared campaign workflow, with a clearer view of the money behind the work. The identity balances creative energy with the precision people expect when dealing with income.

The chosen connection concept is the foundation. Three equal open loops represent creators, agencies and brands. Openings suggest exchange and movement; the shared arrangement makes collaboration central. No role owns the symbol or receives a privileged position.

## Positioning and messaging

- Name in prose: **Creator Cash Flow**. Lowercase only in the logo.
- Short descriptor: **Campaigns, collaborations and cash flow.**
- Brand line: **Good work. Connected.**
- Homepage headline: **Your creativity. A business in motion.**
- Homepage support: **From the first brief to the final invoice. Bring your campaigns, collaborators and cash flow together.**
- Creator message: **More time creating. A clearer view of your income.**
- Agency message: **Keep your creators, campaigns and approvals in sync.**
- Brand message: **Connect the brief, the work and the outcome.**

Use the full name at first contact. CCF is internal shorthand, not a replacement logo. The product’s described capabilities must match what has shipped; example campaign journeys are illustrative until implemented.

## Logo system

The SVG set is an editable geometric reconstruction of the selected visual concept. It is not a pixel trace of the AI-generated lettering. The vector symbol is the consistent source for production applications; the generated board communicates art direction.

| File | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `connection-mark.svg` | Dark symbol on transparent background; use on Paper or Lime |
| `connection-mark-reversed.svg` | Paper symbol on transparent background; use on Ink |
| `logo-primary.svg` | Dark symbol and two-line wordmark; light backgrounds |
| `logo-reversed.svg` | Paper symbol and wordmark; dark backgrounds |
| `app-icon.svg` | Lime square with dark connection symbol; social avatar and app-icon master |
| `brand-tokens.css` | Scoped foundation tokens for designers and developers |

Primary arrangement: symbol left, “creator” above “cash flow.” Keep the two words on the second line together. No tagline in the main logo; place supporting copy separately.

Clear space: leave at least one symbol stroke width around every edge. Prefer twice this distance on busy surfaces. Minimum digital size: symbol 32 px high, complete lockup 180 px wide. At 16–24 px use a dedicated, optically adjusted favicon export after checking its gaps; do not force the full wordmark into a favicon.

Use a single colour for the main mark. A Sage/Paper/Lime split can be used in large supporting diagrams, but never assign permanent colours to the three account roles. Reverse onto a solid Ink field rather than placing a pale mark over a busy photograph.

Do not stretch, rotate, add gradients, add glow, close the loop openings, change stroke ratios, place the mark inside a coin, or join it to an upward arrow. Use the app tile only when a contained icon is needed; do not box the logo everywhere.

The wordmarks contain live text using Manrope, with Arial as a fallback. Install Manrope for consistent rendering; convert wordmark text to outlines before sending final artwork to printers or suppliers. Symbol-only files are font independent. This is identity v2, not a claim of trademark registration or exclusivity.

## Colour

| Name | HEX | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ink | `#252F20` | Text, main logo, dark campaign panels |
| Paper | `#F7F7F2` | Main canvas, reversed logo |
| Lime | `#DEFB82` | Primary actions, selective highlights, app tile |
| Sage | `#A8B698` | Secondary fills and large supporting graphics |
| Muted ink | `#596451` | Supporting text on Paper |
| Hairline | `#DDE2D7` | Nonessential separators |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Raised input and content surfaces |

Aim for roughly 70% Paper/White, 20% Ink, and 10% accents in marketing layouts. This is a composition guide, not a rule for individual components.

Put Ink text on Lime and Sage. Do not use Lime or Sage as small text on Paper. Use labels as well as colour for statuses. Errors and warnings need their own semantic colours; the brand palette is not a complete status system. In dark mode use Ink as the main canvas and Paper for text, retaining Lime for primary actions.

## Typography

Use **Manrope** throughout: 500 for large editorial headlines, 600 for UI headings and actions, 400 for body text, 700 for wordmarks. Fall back to Arial, sans-serif.

| Element | Desktop / mobile | Line height |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Marketing hero | 64 / 40 px | 1.05 |
| Section heading | 36 / 28 px | 1.15 |
| Dashboard heading | 28 / 24 px | 1.2 |
| Body | 16 px | 1.6 |
| UI label | 14 px | 1.4 |
| Supporting detail | 12 px | 1.5 |

Use sentence case. Keep letter spacing neutral for body text; use slightly negative spacing on large headlines. Never stretch lettering to fill a space. Financial values use tabular numerals. Use “R12,000” for rounded marketing examples and “R12,000.00” where cents matter. Write unambiguous dates such as “30 Sep 2026.”

## Photography and graphic language

Show real work: creators filming, editing, recording, planning and collaborating. Prioritise natural light, believable environments, warm skin tones and contemporary South African context. Include people as active professionals, not decoration. Choose varied disciplines and subjects across the image library.

Use one strong image in a hero composition and a second supporting image farther down the page. Keep faces and tools clear of text. Prefer copy beside an image; use an opaque text panel when an overlay is unavoidable. Pair photography with a restrained money or campaign panel rather than layering many floating UI cards.

Use licensed stock for launch; record the source, photographer and permitted uses. The current preview uses cottonbro studio and fauxels imagery from Pexels; these subjects are not clients or endorsers. The updated identity board uses these same stock photographs. No AI-generated people are included in the stock edition. See photo-credits.md for source and licence links.

The open-loop shape may become an oversized, cropped background motif. Limit it to one area per composition. Keep data charts functional; decorative loops should never look like progress indicators.

## Interface language

Use a 4 px spacing foundation: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48 and 64 px. Prefer 12 px panel corners, 8 px inputs and pill-shaped primary actions. Use one strong action per section. Keep landing pages spacious and dashboards compact enough to work.

Primary action: Lime background, Ink label. Secondary action: Paper/transparent surface, Ink label and a visible border. Focus: an explicit Ink outline on light surfaces or Paper outline on dark surfaces, with 3 px offset. Do not remove keyboard focus indicators.

Use thin Lucide-style line icons consistently. Keep meaningful actions labelled. Use photography on landing, onboarding and campaign context; prioritise tasks and records in dense finance screens.

## Voice

Sound clear, warm, capable and grounded. Talk to a working creative professional. Give the next action and its consequence. Avoid “unlock,” “revolutionise,” artificial urgency and promises of guaranteed income.

| Situation | Preferred copy |
| --- | --- |
| Primary entry | Get started |
| Submission | Send for review |
| Approval | Approved. You can schedule your post. |
| Invoice | Invoice sent. Payment is due on 30 Sep. |
| Empty state | Your next campaign starts here. Create a brief or accept an invitation. |
| Error | We couldn’t send your draft. Your work is saved. Try again. |

Only say “Your work is saved” when persistence is confirmed. Distinguish “Approved,” “Invoice sent,” “Payment due” and “Paid”; content approval does not prove payment. Use “Track payment” unless actual payment execution is available.

## Applications

- Website header: primary lockup, generous clear space, no tagline at small sizes.
- Login: primary lockup above the form; creator photography alongside it.
- Social avatar: connection app tile without words; keep essential artwork inside the middle 70% for circular cropping.
- Social post: one headline, one photo, small logo; one clear call to action.
- Email signature: full name and role in text, compact primary logo, site link. No image-only contact details.
- Business card: Ink front with reversed logo; Paper back with contact information in Ink.
- Pitch deck: large editorial titles, Paper backgrounds, Lime for the one important figure or action.

## Handoff

The included SVGs and CSS are reusable local assets. The website and its live logo have not been replaced by this package. The brand board is a visual direction reference; use the SVGs and exact tokens when implementing. Verify small-size output and final print proofs in their intended medium.
