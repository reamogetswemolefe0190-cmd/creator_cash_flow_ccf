# Good work. Connected.

## Purpose

Start qualified partnership conversations with agency decision makers and brand marketing teams. This is an invitation campaign, not a claim that the proposed agency and brand dashboards are already live. No pricing, customer counts, guaranteed returns, payment execution or automatic matching is promised.

Default social call to action: **Message us “PARTNER”.** Publish from an account with a monitored inbox. Flyer call to action: **Explore a partnership**, with the existing homepage address. No unverified booking link has been invented.

Agencies bring perspective. Brands bring ambition. Creators bring the work to life.

We’d like to hear how your team connects those pieces, and where you need more clarity. Swipe through, then message us **PARTNER** to tell us about your next campaign.

Upload carousel-01 through carousel-05 in numeric order, without cropping. Use the same caption across the complete set.

## Reply to an inbound message

Thanks for reaching out. Are you an agency or a brand team? Tell us a little about your next creator campaign: the objective, likely timeline and where you’d most value support. We’ll use that to work out whether there’s a useful next conversation.

## Suggested first run

Start with the agency post and brand post on separate days. Follow with the carousel to explain the conversation, and the landscape creative for LinkedIn. Share the flyer in response to relevant interest. Prioritise qualified conversations over impressions; record role, campaign objective, timeline and agreed next step.
